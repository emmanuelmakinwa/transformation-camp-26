01About Transformation Camp

Transformation
starts here.

Transformation Camp is the annual camp meeting of Transformation Christian Centre.

It is a divine gathering designed to equip, empower and commission believers for God's end-time purpose.

↘
Our mandate

Raise me an
end-time army.

Anchored on the church's foundational mandate, Transformation Camp brings believers, leaders and disciples together in passionate pursuit of God's presence and purpose.

Our vision

Word. Presence.
Power.

Every year, the camp hosts a powerful convergence of uncompromised biblical teaching, fervent worship, prayer, impartation and the demonstration of God's power.

02What to expect

An experience
designed to transform.

Transformation Camp is more than a meeting; it is a divine appointment with God.

01 / THE WORD

Powerful Bible
Teaching

Clear biblical teaching rooted in the uncompromising truth of God's Word, helping participants grow in spiritual maturity, renew their minds and walk confidently in their purpose.

02 / THE PRESENCE

Heartfelt
Worship

A passionate, Christ-centered encounter that draws believers into the manifest presence of God through heartfelt praise and intimate adoration.

03 / THE ALTAR

Fervent Prayer
& Impartation

Spirit-led prayer, impartation and ministry create opportunities to seek God, receive direction and awaken fresh grace for your God-given calling.

04 / THE SUPERNATURAL

Miracles, Signs
& Wonders

We expect the supernatural manifestation of God's power as He confirms His Word through miracles, signs and wonders.

03Transformation stories

Lives
transformed.

These are stories from people whose lives were impacted.

“
During Transformation Camp yesterday, I began experiencing severe pain in my leg due to a leg ulcer. The pain was much worse than usual, and I was in tears. After the program, I went to meet PEM, and he prayed for me. When I woke up this morning and checked my leg, I was no longer limping and the severe pain was gone. I also noticed that the wound had started drying up. To the glory of God, I am healed and recovering.
V
VictoriaLagos, Nigeria
“
During the Day 1 morning session, PEM taught on “The New Man in Christ.” Through the teaching, he helped us understand who we are as new creations — that we are the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus, that we are led by the Spirit of God, that we are seated in heavenly places, and that we are partakers of the divine nature of Christ. I held on to these truths in my spirit, fully convinced of who I am in Christ. After the afternoon session, the pastor prayed for us leaders and instructed us to lay hands on the congregation. As I went to pray for a particular lady and declare the Word of God over her, the accuser began fighting back, telling me that I had no right to be there and questioning who I was. Immediately, I began to declare the Word of God concerning who I am in Christ Jesus. With the confidence I have in Him, I rebuked and cast out the demon. Glory to God! The lady was healed and delivered. Hallelujah!
I
IremideLagos
“
While PEM was teaching on purpose, he said something along the lines of, “God is more interested in fulfilling His purpose in your life than you are anxious about it.” That statement delivered me from anxiety. I thank God.
B
BiblisBenin Republic
“
A friend sent me the link to the program, and I decided to join. At the time, I was experiencing severe breast pain. During the program, I received my healing, and the pain disappeared. Praise the Lord! Hallelujah!
P
PerienceLagos
