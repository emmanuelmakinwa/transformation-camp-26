ABOUT TRANSFORMATION CAMP

Transformation starts here.

Transformation Camp is the annual camp meeting of Transformation Christian Center (TCC), a divine gathering designed to equip, empower, and commission believers for God's end-time purpose.

Anchored on the church's foundational mandate, "Raise Me an End-Time Army," Transformation Camp serves as a sacred meeting place where believers, leaders, and disciples from across the world unite in passionate pursuit of God's presence and purpose.

Each year, the camp hosts a powerful convergence of the uncompromised Word of God, fervent worship, life-transforming teachings, prayer, impartation, and the demonstration of God's power through miracles, signs, and wonders.

Discover the experience →

WHAT TO EXPECT

An experience designed to transform.

Transformation Camp is more than a meeting; it is a divine appointment with God. Every session is intentionally designed to create an atmosphere where lives are transformed, faith is strengthened, and believers are equipped for Kingdom impact.

01

POWERFUL BIBLE TEACHING

At Transformation Camp, every teaching session is rooted in the uncompromising truth of God’s Word and guided by the Holy Spirit. Through clear biblical teaching and practical application, participants are strengthened in faith, renewed in mind, and equipped to grow in spiritual maturity, walk confidently in their God-given purpose, and live the victorious life Christ has made available to every believer.

02

HEARTFELT WORSHIP

At Transformation Camp, worship is a passionate, Christ-centered encounter that draws believers into the manifest presence of God through heartfelt praise and intimate adoration. As we worship, hearts are renewed, burdens are lifted, faith is rekindled, and lives are transformed by the power of His presence.

03

FERVENT PRAYER AND IMPARTATION

Prayer is at the heart of Transformation Camp, creating opportunities for believers to seek God, receive divine direction, and experience His transforming presence. Through Spirit-led prayer, impartation, and ministry, participants are strengthened, empowered, and awakened to fresh grace for their God-given calling.

04

MIRACLES, SIGNS, AND WONDERS

At Transformation Camp, we expect the supernatural manifestation of God’s power as He confirms His Word through miracles, signs, and wonders. Many experience healing, restoration, freedom, renewed hope, and life-changing encounters that reveal the love, mercy, and reality of God’s Kingdom.

TRANSFORMATION STORIES

Lives transformed.

These are stories from people whose lives were impacted.

“

During Transformation Camp yesterday, I began experiencing severe pain in my leg due to a leg ulcer. The pain was much worse than usual, and I was in tears.

After the program, I went to meet PEM, and he prayed for me. When I woke up this morning and checked my leg, I was no longer limping and the severe pain was gone. I also noticed that the wound had started drying up.

To the glory of God, I am healed and recovering.

Victoria Lagos, Nigeria
“

During the Day 1 morning session, PEM taught on “The New Man in Christ.” Through the teaching, he helped us understand who we are as new creations — that we are the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus, that we are led by the Spirit of God, that we are seated in heavenly places, and that we are partakers of the divine nature of Christ.

I held on to these truths in my spirit, fully convinced of who I am in Christ.

After the afternoon session, the pastor prayed for us leaders and instructed us to lay hands on the congregation. As I went to pray for a particular lady and declare the Word of God over her, the accuser began fighting back, telling me that I had no right to be there and questioning who I was.

Immediately, I began to declare the Word of God concerning who I am in Christ Jesus. With the confidence I have in Him, I rebuked and cast out the demon.

Glory to God! The lady was healed and delivered. Hallelujah!

Iremide Lagos
“

While PEM was teaching on purpose, he said something along the lines of, “God is more interested in fulfilling His purpose in your life than you are anxious about it.”

That statement delivered me from anxiety.

I thank God.

Biblis Benin Republic
“

A friend sent me the link to the program, and I decided to join.

At the time, I was experiencing severe breast pain. During the program, I received my healing, and the pain disappeared.

Praise the Lord! Hallelujah!
