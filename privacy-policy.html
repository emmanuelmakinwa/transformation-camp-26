PRIVACY & DATA NOTICE

Your privacy matters to us.

This notice explains how information provided through Transformation Camp registration and partnership forms is handled.

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

How information is collected

This website does not directly collect or store personal information. Registration and partnership information is submitted through Google Forms.

How the information is used

Information submitted through our forms may be accessed by authorised Transformation Camp / Transformation Christian Centre team members for legitimate event-related purposes, including registration administration, communication, participant coordination, and partnership management.

Google Forms

When you choose to register or submit a partnership enquiry, you leave this website and submit your information through a Google Form. Information submitted through Google Forms is processed and stored through Google's services and is subject to Google's applicable privacy practices and terms.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information submitted through our forms. Access to submitted information is limited to authorised individuals who need it for Transformation Camp-related activities. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Your questions

If you have questions about how information submitted through our forms is handled, please contact Transformation Camp through the official contact channels provided on our website.
